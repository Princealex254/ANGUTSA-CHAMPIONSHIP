GETTING STARTED

Register your team

Start from a tournament that is accepting entries. Team registration and player registration happen at different stages.

01

Create your manager account

Choose an open tournament and select Register a Team. Sign in with your existing Team Manager account or create one during registration. Use an email and mobile number you can access for updates.

New manager accounts may remain pending until the competition owner approves the account or the full registration fee is confirmed.

02

Enter your team details

Work through the registration form: choose the competition, enter the team name and home area, add your manager details, and upload an optional team logo. The short code is generated from the team name.

Check that your team name and contact details are correct before continuing.

03

Review, submit, and pay

Review the entry fee and your information, accept the declarations, then submit the registration. Pay the fee separately using the payment instructions shown after submission.

Keep your payment confirmation. The competitions office reviews registrations and payment records; submission does not mean the team is already approved.

04

Wait for approval, then add players

Sign in to the dashboard and check Registrations for progress or any requested action. Player registration becomes available after team approval, and changes can be restricted when a roster is locked.

Register players under the correct team and competition category. Youth records require guardian consent; follow the published rules for eligibility and squad limits.

i

Already have a manager account? Sign in first, then use Create a team in the dashboard. Select an open competition and complete the team registration there.

MANAGER DASHBOARD

Manage your team through the season

Sign in with the manager account you used to register. Use the dashboard navigation to open each area.

TEAM

Teams

Review your teams and their current status. If you manage more than one, choose the team you want to work on using the team selector.

Open Teams in the dashboard →
SQUAD

Keep player records current

Open Squad and choose Add a player to enter a roster record. Use edit actions to correct details, review roster status and consent, and use Show incomplete records to find missing information.

COMPETITION

Track registrations

Open Registrations to check review status and see if anything needs your attention. Read any messages carefully and follow up with the competitions office when requested.

FINANCE

Check payment records

Open Payments to view the fee status and payment history. Payment records are read-only; the finance office issues receipts after reviewing the payment.

MATCH DAY

Follow fixtures and results

Open Fixtures for confirmed matches and recent results. Check the dashboard and your notifications regularly for competition updates.

ACCOUNT

Review rules and profile

Use Rules to read the published requirements for your team's category. Keep your manager contact details current in Profile, and sign out when using a shared device.

NEED A HAND?

We’re here to help.

For registration, account, or dashboard questions, contact the competitions office. Check the published rules before submitting player records.
