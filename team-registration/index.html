Register your team

Complete each step and press Move Next to continue. Required details are checked before the next step opens. View tournaments · Read the rules

Step 1 of 6

Choose the competition

Only competitions with open registration can be selected. Deadlines are enforced by the competitions office.

Step 2 of 6

Team details

Use the name the team is known by. If it is very similar to a team already in the directory, the competitions office will ask for clarification before approval.

Generated automatically from the team name for fixtures and tables.

Player registration takes place later in your manager dashboard, after your team is approved. Any outstanding entry-fee balance should be cleared as soon as possible.
Step 3 of 6

Team Manager account

Sign in with your Team Manager account or create one here. A new account remains pending until the full registration fee is confirmed or the owner approves your account. Contact details are never published on the public website.

Use at least 6 characters.

Sign in with your approved account to use your saved details.

Step 4 of 6

Team logo

Upload your team logo if you have one. If not, continue to the next step.

PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB. If you do not have a logo, continue.

Step 5 of 6

Entry fees and payment

Pay the registration fee by M-Pesa after submitting.

Step 6 of 6

Review and submit

Review your details and confirm the declarations before submitting.

Complete the security check when prompted. A fresh check is required for each protected submission.
