ABOUT THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Our Story, Our Community, Our Game

The Angutsa Football Championship is rooted in Givogi Village, bringing people together through football, celebrating local talent, and promoting the values that make sport meaningful.

OUR PURPOSE

More Than a Football Competition

The Angutsa Football Championship is rooted in Givogi Village, Banja Location, Hamisi Sub-County, Vihiga County.

It is intended to bring teams, players, supporters, and the wider community together through organised football, creating space for sporting opportunity, teamwork, discipline, respect, and fair play.

WHAT GUIDES US

A shared direction for the game

These are proposed guiding statements for the championship, not claims of established achievements.

01

Our Vision

To become a respected platform for football talent, sporting excellence, and community development.

02

To bring people together through organised, competitive, and community-driven football while encouraging teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship.

A NOTE ON OUR PURPOSE

A Message from the Founder

Football is more than a game; it is a powerful force that unites communities, inspires talent, and builds character.

The Angutsa Football Championship is rooted in Givogi Village, Banja Location, Hamisi Sub-County, Vihiga County. My vision is to create a platform that brings people together, celebrates local talent, and promotes teamwork, discipline, and fair play.

I encourage all players, teams, and supporters to embrace the spirit of sportsmanship and make this championship a source of pride for our community.

Together, let us use football to inspire, unite, and create a lasting legacy.

Solomon Angutsa Atsiaya, MBS Founder, The Angutsa Football Championship
